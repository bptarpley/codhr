Jessie Contour

Associate Professor of Practice, Arts and Entertainment Technologies, University of Texas at Austin. Jessie Contour is a creative technologist, designer, animator, educator, and maker. She has worked with AAA video game studios, museums, educational programs, and startups. Within these fields, her focus has been on creating interactive experiences for art, play, and education.

Kelcey Gray

Associate Professor of Practice, Design, University of Texas at Austin. Kelcey Gray is a graphic designer, author, and educator. She is the author of Let’s Make Letters! from PA Press and currently teaches design and typography. Within these fields, her focus has recently been on developing workshops and interactive, narrative-based experiences to increase access to typographic education.
