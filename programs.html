Programs

We are proud to offer the following programs to Texas A&M faculty, staff, and students. Have a project or question? Email us at codhr@tamu.edu.

Consultations

One-on-one guidance on your DH questions, ideas, or projects. Send us an email, or drop by our office hours for a quick conversation — no appointment needed. Office hours are every Tuesday from 3-5 p.m., in-person at Evans Library, 1st floor, in a glass booth to the left of the main service desk. We're also available virtually at https://tamu.zoom.us/my/dh.support. We’ll support you on a first come, first served basis.

Project Support and Development

Technical infrastructure and hands-on support, from a first idea through design and hosting. Check out our current portfolio of projects.

DH Speaker Series

Guest speakers from across the discipline, brought in throughout the year. See our gallery of past speakers.

DH Collective

A casual space for project talks and open conversation among Texas A&M's DH community. Join our DH Collective mailing list to receive information about our meetings.

DH Digest Newsletter

A weekly roundup of DH news, opportunities, and events from the global DH community, sent straight to your inbox. Join our mailing list to receive the DH Digest and periodic updates about CoDHR.
